Frequently Asked Questions

Is this a genuine Microsoft 365 license?
Yes. You receive a genuine Microsoft 365 Personal license key that you activate directly on microsoft.com. It is the same product you would buy from the Microsoft Store, with no restrictions.
Can I use it on my existing Microsoft account?
Yes. You activate the license on your existing Microsoft account at microsoft.com/setup. You do not need to create a new account.
How many devices can I install it on?
Microsoft 365 Personal allows you to install the fully featured apps on up to 5 devices simultaneously — PC, Mac, tablet, and phone.
What if I already have Microsoft 365 through my university?
University licenses are tied to your student email and expire when you graduate. A personal license is yours indefinitely and is not linked to your institution, making it a better long-term investment.
