What is Figma Professional?

Figma is a browser-based interface design and prototyping tool that has become the global standard for product designers, UX researchers, and development teams. The Professional plan removes the project and version history limits of the free plan, making it suitable for serious product work. Whether you are designing an app, building a pitch deck mockup, or wireframing a new feature, Figma Professional is the tool used by every product team that takes design seriously.

Unlimited Projects
No cap on the number of files or projects. Organize all your design work across clients, courses, and products without restriction.
Full Version History
Access the complete history of every file — restore any previous version, see every change, and review who made what edits and when.
Team Collaboration
Work alongside developers and co-founders in real-time. Leave comments, share prototypes, and hand off designs with inspect-ready specs.
Advanced Prototyping
Build clickable, animated prototypes that look and behave like real apps — no code required. Share a link for user testing or investor demos.
Design Systems
Create shared component libraries and style guides that keep your product visually consistent as it scales.
FigJam Included
FigJam is Figma's online whiteboard for brainstorming, user flows, and team workshops. Included with every Professional seat.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need an existing Figma account?
No. If you do not have a Figma account, create a free one at figma.com before submitting your request. Use that email when filling in the form. We will upgrade the account associated with that address.
Will my existing files and projects be preserved?
Yes. Upgrading your account to Professional does not affect your existing files. All your current work remains exactly as it is, and you gain access to additional features on top of it.
Is Figma Professional the same as a Figma Organization plan?
No. Figma Professional is the individual paid tier — it gives you unlimited projects, full version history, and advanced prototyping. The Organization plan is an enterprise tier for large companies and is not what is being offered here.
Can I use Figma Professional for commercial client work?
Yes. A Figma Professional account allows you to use Figma for personal, academic, and commercial projects with no restrictions on the output you create.
